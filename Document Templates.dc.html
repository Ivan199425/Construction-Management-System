DOCUMENT CONTROL — PROJECT FORMS
Rev 0  ·  A4  ·  AUD, GST 10%

Project document templates

Nine forms on the Cubic letterhead, ready to print or fill in on screen. Each carries a document reference, revision, project and contract number, the signature blocks that form needs, and a page footer. Open one, edit the text directly, then export to PDF.

CCM-SC · 2 pages
Subcontract Agreement
Formal instrument: parties, scope, subcontract sum, retention, programme, insurances, execution. Your terms and conditions attach as Annexure A.
CCM-INV · 1 page
Tax Invoice / Progress Claim
Claim-to-date breakdown, variations, retention and deductions, GST summary, bank details and a Security of Payment notice line.
CCM-VO · 1 page
Variation Order
Priced variation with labour / material / plant breakdown, O&P, adjusted contract sum and dual signature acceptance.
CCM-SI · 1 page
Site Instruction
Written direction with priority, cost and time implications, and an acknowledgement signature for the subcontractor's copy.
CCM-RFI · 2 pages
Request for Information
Query on the contract documents with drawing references, programme and cost impact, and a full response page for the consultant.
CCM-EOT · 2 pages
Extension of Time Claim
Delay notice with event particulars, critical-path impact table, days claimed, delay costs and a Superintendent determination panel.
CCM-PR · 2 pages
Monthly Progress Report
Status dashboard, work done and planned, delays, cost and variations, WHS statistics, quality, risks and four site photo slots.
CCM-HOC · 2 pages
Project Handover Certificate
Certifies Practical Completion and starts the DLP — statutory certificates, as-builts, keys and meters, commercial position, and dual e-signature.
CCM-DEF · landscape A4
Defect List Report
Eight defects per sheet — location, description, trade, priority, dates, status, verification and a drag-and-drop photo cell per item. Open / closed / total counters in the header.
Before first use
Check the letterhead details and the department email on each form. Confirm the Security of Payment wording for the state you build in. Paste your terms and conditions in as Annexure A to the subcontract, and have a lawyer review the special conditions before issue.
